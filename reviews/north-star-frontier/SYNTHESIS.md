# Nucleus + gatehouse against the North Star — synthesis (2026-09-08)

North Star: *"Nucleus should continuously expand the frontier of safely delegatable machine agency: any model should be able to do as much useful real-world work as its principal is willing to authorize, while being structurally incapable of exceeding that authorization."*

Inputs: six internal maps, five SOTA reports, five per-clause gap analyses and five skeptical verifications (40 candidate gaps, 25 verifier-found missed gaps). Static reading only; every file:line below was re-read by a verifier. Both repos unmodified.

---

## 1. Where nucleus + gatehouse stand today

Nucleus has built the hard, rarely-built middle of the North Star and almost none of its edges. Behind the tool-proxy there is a genuinely structural core — a sealed effect home whose spawn/fetch/broker paths consume an `Authority` minted only by a discharge pipeline, a cap-std file sandbox, a Biscuit-shaped Ed25519 delegation chain with monotone meet re-verified per hop, a node-side budget ledger, Firecracker + jailer + netns default-deny with in-guest probes, a witnessed hash-chained lineage, and an unusually honest ledger discipline (ratchets that only shrink, demotions recorded with evidence) — with extracted-Lean and Kani coverage that no comparable runtime has. But the boundary *around the model* is cooperative and single-vendor in every tier (the agent runs on the host under one CLI's bypass flag, held by a hook that lives in a private repo), the fine-grained decision point runs *inside* the guest it polices, the shipped exec tool cannot deserialise at the proxy, credentialed real-world APIs (git push, PRs, cloud, payments) have no reachable path, approvals are op-name counters that the HTTP path never consults (#2406) and that record no approver, there is no revocation for any issued credential, `SinkScope` is minted `unrestricted()` everywhere so the carrier's only per-target attenuation is unreachable, and the one live-pod measurement on record is a `codegen` pod that could neither read nor write. Every ratchet in the tree is monotone in containment or proof hygiene; nothing measures utility under authorization, and the one instrument that could (flow-replay) removes the lattice by design. Gatehouse is the mirror image: a Lean-sound row-local checker decides `Cap ≤ ceiling` for proof-carrying plans and a refutable receipt/log discipline is DECIDED, but nothing enforces the Cap at runtime, the ceiling is an unsigned `let` behind one shared bearer, and there are no witnesses. Clause 4 (structural) is the best-served clause and even it is proved about mirrors under a cooperative outer boundary; clauses 1, 2, 3 and 5 are asserted, well-designed in places, and largely unwired or unmeasured. The good news is that most gaps are "expose, wire and measure" over a sound core rather than "rebuild"; the two exceptions are the inverted fence (agent outside, PDP inside) and the missing mandate/approval object, which are architectural.

## 3. Architectural themes

### T1 — Invert the fence: agent inside, proved supervisor outside
Gaps: N-01, N-02, N-19, N-20, N-21 (residual `run` path), N-18 (shell egress).
Target: the agent harness — any vendor CLI, SDK loop or open-weight runner — is the pod's `WorkloadSpec`, running in the guest under a distinct uid with Landlock (paths compiled from `PathLattice`), seccomp, and a mount namespace, whose only exits are a stdio/Unix-socket MCP channel to the proxy and loopback. The fine-grained decision point (`Kernel::decide` + flow graph + budget + revocation set) moves to a host-side supervisor in `nucleus-node` over the existing broker vsock channel, fronting a TLS-terminating egress proxy (host+method+path ACLs, credential injection) and an always-on allowlist DNS forwarder; the in-guest proxy becomes a thin client holding the public attenuation token and is kept as defence in depth. Tier 1 becomes the same supervisor with Landlock + seccomp + user-netns on the host and no shared secrets in the agent's env. `nucleus run` spawns nothing on the host except the pod; the vendor launch path moves to `examples/vendor-integrations/`.
SOTA: Sandlock split-TCB (static kernel policy carries authority, narrow supervisor carries decisions, TOCTOU-safe execve), Firecracker design doc ("all guest egress is untrusted, filter at the host"), Codex cloud / E2B / Kubernetes Agent Sandbox / Anthropic sandbox-runtime all placing the agent inside the fence, Landlock ABI v6/v7 + seccomp-notify, `guest_memfd` secret hiding.

### T2 — One authorization object: the mandate chain
Gaps: N-05, N-06, N-07, N-08, N-09, N-10, N-12, N-13.
Target: `LatticeCertificate` becomes the single carrier of everything a principal means. Blocks gain (a) a small, total, decidable *caveat* language over the `ActionTerm` (op, path glob, host glob, git-ref glob, argv prefix, counterparty, per-op count/window, time window, "requires approval signed by kid K") whose meet is conjunction, so monotonicity stays provable; (b) one semantic glob-subsumption primitive (gatehouse's `globSubsumes`, golden-fixtured) replacing three containment semantics and the syntactic `PathLattice::meet`; (c) `SinkScope`/caveats authored from `ProfileSpec` and `nucleus token delegate --scope`; (d) co-signed/third-party blocks with `trusting` scoping and a `min_signers` caveat so an approver, budget authority or second principal contributes a hop without the root key; (e) `revocation_id = block.signature`, a `RevocationSet` input to `verify_certificate`, signed `Revoke` messages pushed over the existing lockdown stream, and a node epoch as the kill-switch; (f) a node-signed `allocation_id` naming the ledger allocation, per-op/per-window/per-sink budget limits. An *approval* is then a signed block bound to `args_hash` from the `ActionTerm`, verified by the pinned-key logic `ApprovalBundle` already has, consumed exactly once by `decide_term`, and emitted as a lineage edge; `grant_approval(op, n)` and the counter registry are deleted. Grant ingress converts foreign objects (OAuth/RAR, ID-JAG, `act` chains, AP2 Intent, rule files) into the root block with the source hash as provenance.
SOTA: Biscuit v5 (offline attenuation, third-party blocks, `trusting`, per-block revocation ids, seal), UCAN v1 delegation/invocation split with `pol` arg policies, Macaroon what/who discharges, AP2 Intent → Cart mandates, AuthZEN AARP, PORTICO epoch-bound grants, RFC 9396 / 8693 at the edges, IETF attenuating-agent-tokens (quotas in shared state + allocation reference).

### T3 — The effect surface as a typed, growable, receipted inventory
Gaps: N-03, N-04, N-15, N-16, N-17, N-18 (DNS/proxy half), N-31.
Target: one `nucleus-api-types` crate shared by the HTTP handlers and both MCP faces; a typed effect vocabulary in the tool schema (`GitPush{remote,ref}`, `CreatePr{repo,base}`, `Http{method,host,path}`, `ToolCall{server,name,args}`, `EmailSend{to}`, …) so the effect type is chosen by the tool invoked, not inferred from argv; a generic `/v1/tool` route that mediates any third-party MCP `tools/call` through the kernel with its `SinkClass` and argument caveats; `egress` exposed as an MCP tool and SDK method with method/path ACLs, per-upstream budgets, optional RFC 8693 exchange, and an in-guest git-credential helper so `git push`/`gh pr create` are real Tier-2 effects; `spawn_agent` as a real effect (sub-pod with attenuated cert *and* the parent's admitted binary), per-subagent task tokens; per-value labels via a quarantined typed-extraction tool so research-then-edit can end in a commit; edit/delete/mkdir, streaming `run`, certificate renewal, persisted memory. ### T4 — Protocol-shaped edges so "any model" is a testable property

## 6. Open questions for the maintainer

1. **Which side of the fence is the product?** README:145 calls the host-side runner "an integration example"; `docs/plugin-surface.md` calls the in-guest `workload` "the agent seam". Is "agent inside the pod" (Q-1) the intended Tier-2 shape, or is the host runner + private hook the shipping product with the guest seam reserved for probes? N-01 is a delete in one case and a build in the other.
2. **Must the fine gates survive guest compromise?** L-1 (host-side supervisor) is an epic. Is the accepted trust model "guest kernel LPE = pod compromise, bounded by netns + Never" (then N-02 is a documented residual), or must taint/budget/egress decisions hold against a compromised guest (then L-1 is on the critical path)?
3. **Where does foreign-authority translation live?** CLAUDE.md assigns vendor-specific translation to the orchestrator. Does grant ingress for *standard* objects (OAuth/RAR, ID-JAG, `act` chains, AP2 mandates, rule files) belong in nucleus (L-4), or stay orchestrator-side with nucleus publishing only the lattice schema?
4. **Caveat language choice.** Bespoke total predicate language (smallest, provable in-house), a Biscuit-Datalog subset (interop, conformance vectors), or Cedar (already Lean-verified, bridge exists, different data model)? This fixes the shape of Q-2 and the proof obligation in L-6.
5. **Freeze policy on `Operation`.** Keep the 13-variant enum frozen and grow via `extensions` (property-tested only, 72-site Kani divergence), or re-extract with a larger enum given the Aeneas 13-variant `repr(u8)` limit already hit? This decides whether `EmailSend`/`CloudMutation`/`ToolCall` can ever be PROVED.
6. **Who is the approver in `nucleus run`?** The host's `approval_signing_key.der`, a WebAuthn/device key, an IdP-issued CIBA result, or an operator roster? N-05/N-13 need one answer; today it is the node.
7. **Revocation delivery.** Deny-list over the lockdown stream (needs the stream up), node epoch bump (coarse, no list), short TTL + `/v1/renew` heartbeat (no offline revocation at all), or all three layered? Which deployments have offline verifiers that must honour a revocation?
8. **Canonical utility corpus.** AgentDojo suites (network + license in CI), the in-repo flow-replay corpus (155 lines, no model), or an in-VM SWE-bench-style task set? `frontier.json`'s meaning depends on it, as does the `corpus_steps_GUARD` pin.
9. **Model matrix reachability.** Which backends can CI reach (secrets, cost caps), and is an open-weight OpenAI-compatible endpoint available for the nightly? Without it clause 1 stays unmeasured.
10. **Nucleus × gatehouse scope.** Gatehouse is proprietary; is unification (gates as pods, shared lattice, `Cap` embedding proof) in scope for the OSS repo, and where does the bridge crate live?
11. **Baseline policy.** Adopt the North Star ledger's same-change pinning for the exemplar scoreboard (removes human-lowered baselines and today's 12-sorry / 575-theorem slack)? Should the committed `scoreboard.json` be CI-written or deleted?
12. **Docs truth pass.** Authorise one PR correcting `verified-claims.md`, `NORTH_STAR.md`, `notes/VERIFIED_CLAIMS.md`, `docs/PROOFS.md`, `CONJECTURES.md` (total row), `production-delta.md`, `envelope/lib.rs`, `cred-broker/lib.rs`, `wimse-aims-conformance-gap.md`, `SECURITY_TODO.md §8`, or keep the adversarial reviews as dated point-in-time artefacts?
13. **Tier 1's future.** Keep `nucleus run --local` as a product (then Landlock/seccomp/user-netns and principal-held approval keys are required, N-19) or demote it to dev-only with a printed warning and no safety claim?
14. **Covert channels in the sentence.** `docs/north-star.md` excludes timing/cache/microarchitectural channels; should the five-clause sentence carry that exclusion explicitly, and should DNS-tunnel/domain-fronting within an allowed host be listed as a residual rather than left unmentioned?
15. **Budget ownership.** Should nucleus meter LLM spend itself (a metering proxy for the model API on the credentialed-egress path) so `max_cost_usd` is structural for every model, or is LLM spend explicitly the orchestrator's (then clause 3 should say so)?
